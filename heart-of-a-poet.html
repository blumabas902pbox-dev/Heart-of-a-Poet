Heart of a Poet

By Bryan Jay M.

Scroll softly to read

My ink is thick, my lava lamp is low,

These treasure seeds I've got to sow.

I weave in the shadows, I paint the light,

And send my soul into a restless night.

I build a futuristic city, word by word

It's love brought sword against sword.

Hoping one day my voice is finally heard,

From the heart of a poet, of its herd.

But sometimes these rooms stays still,

No matter how much efforts I spill.

They skim the lines and miss the ache,

Such little dreams I bend and break.

I’m tucked away on dusty old shelf,

Just alone, talking mostly to myself.

It’s lonely that poets are so underrated,

Where every rhyme went understated.

An imperfect beat, for a simple song,

For those hearts who never stay for long.

But then I pause a bit and breathe it in,

The way the paper meets my glossy pen.

I don’t need a crowd to see the spark,

To keep on dancing through neon dark.

For even if the digital world is blind,

To a magic I have for you gets left behind,

Our love might impossible as it seems,

But it always find the way, we redeem.

The joys in every writers story to be told,

A lovin' so pure of heart is made of gold.
